## Deskripsi
High-speed semester billing grid with virtualized rows, instant hover tooltips, and side-drawer manual payment input.

## Detail Teknis
- **`pages/admin/SppPage.tsx` (Virtualized 6-Month Billing Grid)**:
  - Implement **Virtual Scrolling (`@tanstack/react-virtual`)** for table rows (students) to maintain 60fps performance even with thousands of students.
  - Controls: Year selector, semester selector (Ganjil Jul-Des / Genap Jan-Jun), class filter, and **Comfort vs Dense View Toggle**.
  - Table Structure: Rows = students, Columns = 6 semester months.
  - **PTDARRAHMAN Color-Coded Cells**: Emerald Green `#22C55E` (paid), Gold/Amber `#D4A853` (partial/cicilan), Crimson Red `#FF3B30` (unpaid).
  - **Interactive Floating Tooltips**: Hovering over any cell displays an instant lightweight tooltip preview (Payer name, payment date, method, and receipt number) without needing a click.
- **Split-Pane / Side-Drawer Payment Workflow**:
  - Clicking a student name or unpaid cell opens the **Slide-Over Payment Drawer (`SppPaymentDrawer.tsx`)** on the right side of the screen.
  - Displays the student's full SPP history and an inline **"Input Bayar Manual" form** with Quick-Chip nominal buttons. Admin can submit payments for multiple students consecutively without ever leaving or reloading the grid!
- **Export & Print**: Dedicated print-friendly `@media print` styling (clean ivory/white layout) and Excel export button.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Grid renders with virtual scrolling (`@tanstack/react-virtual`) with zero frame drops
- [ ] Hovering over cells shows instant tooltip with payment metadata
- [ ] Clicking student/cell opens slide-over payment drawer without page redirect
- [ ] Color coding aligns with PTDARRAHMAN palette (Emerald, Gold, Crimson)
- [ ] Print and export functions work accurately
