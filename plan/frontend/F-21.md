## Deskripsi
Void/cancel payment.

## Detail Teknis
In payment detail view (wherever payments are shown), add "Batalkan" button (danger style). Click → Modal: show payment info, textarea for reason (required), confirm button. On confirm → POST /payments/:id/void → show success toast → refresh data. Voided payments shown with strikethrough and "DIBATALKAN" badge. Voided receipts show watermark/stamp.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Void button visible on confirmed payments
- [ ] Modal with required reason
- [ ] After void: payment shows cancelled state
- [ ] Receipt reflects void status
