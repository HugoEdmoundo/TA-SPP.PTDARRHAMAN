## Deskripsi
Dual-format receipt presentation: shareable digital receipt cards for instant WhatsApp sharing and formal printable PDFs for school administration.

## Detail Teknis
- **`components/ReceiptViewerModal.tsx` (Liquid Glass Dual Viewer)**:
  - Tab 1: **Kartu Digital Bukti Bayar (`ReceiptShareCard.tsx`)** — optimized for mobile viewing and social sharing. Uses PTDARRAHMAN Ivory/Emerald styling, QR code verification, and a prominent **"1-Klik Kirim ke WhatsApp / Simpan PNG"** button.
  - Tab 2: **Formal PDF Viewer** — embeds formal institutional PDF receipt in iframe/canvas for accounting archival or formal printing.
  - Features: Print button (triggers print-friendly `@media print` CSS or window.print), copy receipt link, and WhatsApp direct dispatch.
  - Usage: Integrated across Admin payment details, Wali payment history, and checkout success pages.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] ReceiptShareCard renders cleanly with 1-click WhatsApp share and PNG export
- [ ] Institutional PDF renders accurately and supports direct printing
- [ ] Both formats prominently display receipt number, date, student NIS, and paid amount
- [ ] Seamlessly accessible in both Admin and Wali layouts
