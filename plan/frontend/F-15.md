## Deskripsi
Frictionless checkout flow with real-time rolling balance animations, quick-chip infaq additions, and WhatsApp-ready shareable receipts.

## Detail Teknis
- **`components/wali/CheckoutBottomSheet.tsx` (Slide-Up Mobile Checkout)**:
  - Slide-up bottom sheet reviewing selected SPP/Non-SPP/Event items.
  - **Reactive Number Rolling Animation**: Total balance counter animates smoothly upwards/downwards in real-time as items are toggled or infaq is modified.
  - **Quick-Chip Infaq Input**: Utilizes `InputCurrency.tsx` paired with interactive chips (`[+Rp 10.000]`, `[+Rp 50.000]`, `[+Rp 100.000]`, `[+Rp 250.000]`). Parents can add voluntary infaq in 1 tap without typing zeroes.
  - Action: Prominent Emerald Green button "Bayar Sekarang" → POST /my/checkout → seamless redirect to payment gateway (Midtrans/Xendit/etc.).
- **`pages/wali/PaymentSuccessPage.tsx` (Satisfying Celebration & Share Card)**:
  - Triggers satisfying checkmark animation and lightweight GPU-friendly confetti celebration upon landing.
  - **WhatsApp-Ready Receipt Card (`ReceiptShareCard.tsx`)**: Displays a beautifully formatted digital receipt (vertical 9:16 or square aspect ratio) in PTDARRAHMAN Ivory/Emerald palette, featuring school crest, student NIS, itemized payment, and QR verification stamp.
  - Prominent Action: **"1-Klik Kirim ke WhatsApp / Simpan Bukti (PNG)"** button using `html-to-image` or Web Share API, allowing parents to instantly report payment to spouses or school admins.
- **`pages/wali/PaymentFailedPage.tsx`**: Clear error explanation with "Coba Lagi" button and support WhatsApp link.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Number rolling animation updates smoothly in real-time during checkout
- [ ] Quick-chip buttons add infaq nominals accurately without keyboard typing
- [ ] Payment success page renders ReceiptShareCard with 1-click WhatsApp share / image download
- [ ] Satisfying checkmark and lightweight celebration animation trigger on success
