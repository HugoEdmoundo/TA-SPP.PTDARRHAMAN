## Deskripsi
Admin catat pembayaran langsung. Cash atau transfer manual.

## Detail Teknis
`app/routes/payments.py`.
POST `/payments/manual` — body: {student_id, items: [{type, month/year or bill_id, amount}], method (cash/transfer), infaq_amount, notes}.
Creates Payment record(s). Updates bill status. Auto-generates receipt. Admin-only.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Admin can record cash/transfer payments
- [ ] Bill status correctly updated
- [ ] Receipt auto-generated
- [ ] Supports partial payments (cicilan)
