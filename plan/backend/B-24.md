## Deskripsi
Multiple report endpoints with PDF/Excel export.

## Detail Teknis
`app/routes/reports.py`.
GET `/reports/spp-semester?year=2025&semester=1&format=json|pdf|excel`.
GET `/reports/monthly?month=7&year=2025&format=json|pdf|excel`.
GET `/reports/student/:id?format=json|pdf`.
GET `/reports/infaq?year=2025&format=json|excel`.
GET `/reports/events?format=json|pdf|excel`.
Use `openpyxl` for Excel, `reportlab`/`fpdf2` for PDF.
