## Deskripsi
Endpoint untuk link wali ke siswa. Wali bisa lihat daftar anak.

## Detail Teknis
POST `/students/:id/parents` (link parent_id to student).
DELETE `/students/:id/parents/:parent_id` (unlink).
GET `/students/:id/parents` (list parents of student).
GET `/my/children` (wali sees own children — uses JWT to identify).
Admin-only for linking, wali for `/my/children`.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Admin can link/unlink parents to students
- [ ] Wali can see their linked children
